# Seats, tables & stacks
> The rules of the game around the market.

## Seats

- A seat is **10 tUSDC**: free on the house, or from your own bankroll.
- Your **stack** starts at 10. Each round your whole stack rides your pick. Win and it multiplies at the price you took; lose and you fall. You can never lose more than your seat.
- If part of a budget can't be filled, that remainder stays in your stack. It was never at risk.

## Tables

- Seats are grouped into **tables** of up to **8**. A table fills for 15 seconds, then seals, and plays until one run is left standing.
- A table with one player is a solo climb.

## Picking

- Pick **UP** or **DOWN** in the first **35 seconds** of a round. A pick made while you're already riding queues for the next round, so there's no dead time between minutes.
- No pick means you sit the round out and your stack is untouched.

## When a run ends

- **You bail:** you keep your stack.
- **You fall:** your side was wrong at the bell.
- **The sweep:** a stack under **1 tUSDC** can't climb back, so it's cashed out.
- **You walk away:** a seat with no pick for **10 rounds** is banked automatically. Its stack is cash, so you keep all of it.

## Ranked and practice

- **Ranked** seats are paid from your own tUSDC bankroll, which needs a wallet and a signed deposit. Only ranked runs count on the boards. Each wallet gets **20 ranked seats a day**, reset at 00:00 UTC.
- **Practice** seats are free and staked by the house. They play the same market, but they never count on the boards and their winnings never credit a balance. They're capped too: at most 12 practice runs alive at once, and practice pauses if the house balance runs low.

## The weekly boards

Boards reset every **Monday 00:00 UTC**. A run counts in the week its last bell rang. Your wallet is your identity on the boards, so two browsers on one wallet are one climber.

- **The Summit:** net winnings this week (what your ranked runs paid out, minus what they cost).
- **Longest Climb:** the most rounds one ranked run survived this week.

The all-time ranked board sits under them on [the boards page](/leaders).

## The wall goes to sleep

If nobody is seated and nobody has the page open for 10 minutes, the game stops opening rounds to save gas. The first visitor wakes it, and the next round strikes at the top of the minute.
