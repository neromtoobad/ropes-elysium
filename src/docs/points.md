# Points, handles & invites
> Season 0 on testnet: how ROPES Points are scored, the leagues, your X handle on the boards, and invite links.

## What scores

Only **ranked** seats score: seats paid from your own tUSDC bankroll. Practice seats play the same market on the house's money and never count, same rule as the weekly boards.

| What | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Every ranked seat that ends | +10 |
| Every round that seat survived | +5 |
| Banking a profit | +20 per 1× above the buy-in, up to +200 |
| Last one holding at the table | +100 |
| Your first ranked seat of each UTC day | +25 |
| Weekly Summit, top 10 (net winnings above zero) | +500, 300, 200, 150, 125, 100, 75, 60, 50, 40 |
| Weekly Longest Climb, top 3 | +150, 100, 50 |
| Your invites | 10% of everything they earn |

A run scores the moment its last bell rings. Weekly placings land when the week closes, Monday 00:00 UTC. The cap of 20 ranked seats per wallet per day applies, so points cannot be bought by volume alone.

Points can also be granted by hand, for things like a confirmed bug report or an event prize. Those show up the same way.

## Live, not snapshots

The table is computed straight from the ranked ledger every 30 seconds. **Last 24h** is what each climber earned in the past 24 hours, and the ▲▼ arrows are how far they moved compared with where they stood exactly a day ago. **NEW** means they weren't on the table a day ago.

## Your handle

On the [points page](/points) you can put your X handle next to your wallet. It shows on the points table and the weekly boards instead of your seat name. One handle per wallet, one wallet per handle.

Handles are self-claimed. ROPES doesn't verify them, so before anything is paid out to a handle, the winner proves it by posting or messaging from that account.

## Invites

Your invite link is on the points page: `playropes.xyz/r/YOURCODE`. When someone opens it, their browser keeps the code until they make their first deposit, and then their wallet is attached to yours. From then on you get 10% of the points they earn. They lose nothing.

- An invite counts only for a **new** wallet: one with no more than 3 ranked seats.
- Once attached, it never changes.
- Your own link, or a wallet you invited inviting you back, doesn't count.

## Fair play

Season 0 is testnet. Points are your standing, not a token or a promise of one. Wallets that farm (a ring of wallets inviting each other, scripted seats, or anything built to game the table rather than play it) get removed from the table and any prize.
