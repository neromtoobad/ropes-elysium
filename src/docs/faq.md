# FAQ
> Short answers to the questions people ask first.

**Is this real money?**
Not yet. Testnet uses tUSDC, a test dollar with no value. Mainnet with real USDC follows Elysium mainnet (Nov 9) and a security review.

**Do I need a wallet?**
No. Take a free seat and play the same on-chain market on the house.

**Can I lose more than my seat?**
No. The most you can lose is the stake you put in.

**Who sets the price?**
The contract, on every trade, from the Hyperliquid mark. See [Pricing](/docs/pricing).

**Where do the strike and close come from?**
The open of Hyperliquid's own 1-minute candle. Anyone can check them. See [Price source & audit](/docs/price-source) and [/transparency](/transparency).

**What happens if the price feed fails at the bell?**
The round voids and everyone gets back exactly what they paid.

**What's the house edge?**
The spread: you buy at fair value + 1% and sell at fair value − 1%. There's no other fee.

**Why can't I trade the contract directly?**
Until prices come straight from HyperCore, trading is operator-only so nobody can front-run the price relay. It opens to everyone after the precompile ships.

**Is there a token?**
Not yet. $ROPES launches on Ascend after mainnet. Anything else claiming to be ROPES is a scam.

**Who builds ROPES?**
[neromtoobad](https://nerom.site), solo. Code is open: [github.com/neromtoobad/ropes-elysium](https://github.com/neromtoobad/ropes-elysium).
