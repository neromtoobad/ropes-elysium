# Quickstart
> Play your first round in two minutes, with or without a wallet.

## Practice (no wallet)

1. Open [playropes.xyz/play](https://playropes.xyz/play).
2. Choose a climber and type a name.
3. Tap **PRACTICE**. The house stakes 10 tUSDC for you.
4. When the clock says **BETS OPEN**, tap **▲ UP** or **▼ DOWN**. Entries close 35 seconds into the minute.
5. Watch your climber. Tap **BAIL** at any second to take what the market pays right now, or ride to the bell.

Practice seats play the same real on-chain market, but they never count on the boards and their winnings don't credit a balance.

## Ranked (your own wallet)

1. Add **Elysium Testnet** to your wallet:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Network name | Elysium Testnet |
| RPC | `https://testnet-rpc.elysium.kinetiq.xyz` |
| Chain ID | `99801` |
| Currency | HYPE |
| Explorer | `https://elysium.kinetiq.xyz/testnet-explorer` |

2. Get a little testnet HYPE for gas from the [Elysium faucet](https://elysium.kinetiq.xyz/testnet-faucet). A transaction costs about 0.00001 HYPE.
3. On the **Wallet** page, tap **GET TEST tUSDC**. That mints 10,000 test dollars to your wallet.
4. **Deposit** what you want to play with. Ranked seats are 10 tUSDC each and come out of your bankroll with no wallet popup. Each wallet gets **20 ranked seats a day** (resets 00:00 UTC).
5. Tap **RANKED** and play as above. Winnings go back to your bankroll, and the run counts on [the weekly boards](/leaders). **WITHDRAW ALL** sends everything back to your wallet.

> tUSDC is test money with no value. It exists only on Elysium testnet.
