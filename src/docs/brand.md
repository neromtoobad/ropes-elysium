# Brand kit
> How to write and show ROPES.

## Name

**ROPES**, in capitals. The tagline is *one minute of Hyperliquid, on a rope*. The token is **$ROPES**.

## Official channels

- X: [@PlayRopes](https://x.com/PlayRopes)
- Site: [ropes.nerom.site](https://ropes.nerom.site)
- Code: [github.com/neromtoobad/ropes-elysium](https://github.com/neromtoobad/ropes-elysium)

We never DM first and never ask for keys or seed phrases.

## Colours

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Night | `#0d0a1c` | background |
| Panel | `#161329` | cards |
| UP | `#00e58a` | UP, winning, live |
| DOWN | `#ff2f52` | DOWN, falling |
| Gold | `#ffc94d` | the line to beat, money, highlights |
| Text | `#eeecf5` | body |
| Dim | `#8d8ab0` | secondary text |

UP is always green, DOWN is always red, and the line to beat is always gold.

## Type

- **Russo One** for headlines, in capitals.
- **Chakra Petch** for body text.
- **JetBrains Mono** for numbers, addresses and labels.

## Signature art

![The ROPES climber at the top of the minute](/brand/signature.png)

The hooded climber at the top of a rope, as the minute dial reaches 60 and the bell is about to ring. It's the key art for posts, link previews and the anti-scam closer. Download: [`/brand/signature.png`](/brand/signature.png).

## Post frame

Every ROPES image uses the same frame: the logo top-left, a tag chip top-right (green for announcements and proof, gold for build logs and roadmaps, violet for explainers, red for safety), and the rope footer strip with ropes.nerom.site and @PlayRopes. Headlines are Russo One in capitals; one word in green, gold or red carries the point.

## Logo

The knot on a rope with a candle in its heart. Files are in the repo under `public/` (`logo-full.png`, `mark.webp`, `icon.png`).
