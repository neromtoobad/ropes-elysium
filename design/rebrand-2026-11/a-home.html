how it works

Three steps, all onchain.

01

Pick an expiry and strike

New series open every 5 minutes, 15 minutes and hour. Strikes sit on a grid around the live price.

02

Buy or sell

Orders rest and match in the contract. Buyers pay the premium. Sellers lock their maximum loss. No liquidations.

03

Settle on Hyperliquid

At expiry the contract reads Hyperliquid's price and pays out. Every settlement is public on the transparency page.
